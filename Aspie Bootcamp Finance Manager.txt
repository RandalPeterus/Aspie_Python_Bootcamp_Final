Aspie Bootcamp Finance Manager:

Used to track finances, with a time of input tracked. 
Runs on Python, no external libraries required.

How to use: 
Once open, input 1 or 2 and press enter to select the appropriate first financial transaction.
Enter the name, then the amount, then the time will be recorded automatically, and you will be returned to the starting menu. 
Repeat until all transactions are recorded. 
Use 3 to view all of your transactions with timestamps
Use 4 to see total income, expenses, and the net balance.
